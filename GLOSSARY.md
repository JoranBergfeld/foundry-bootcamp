# Winter Bootcamp

Six one-hour sessions about building, evaluating, deploying, integrating, and governing an agent.

## Language

**Agent**:
The subject participants build, evaluate, deploy, integrate, and govern during the bootcamp.

**Lab**:
The 40-45-minute follow-along learning unit within one bootcamp session, with participant documentation and any supporting executable code. Its documentation supports following the material without the presenter.
_Avoid_: MicroHack (when referring to an individual bootcamp lab)

**Independent lab**:
A lab that can be attended without completing any other lab. It may rely on the shared participant environment and baseline dataset, but not on another lab's completion or outputs.

**Content pack**:
The unit imported into the MicroHack platform, containing participant and coach material. The bootcamp's content pack contains all six labs.
_Avoid_: Lab (when referring to the platform import unit)

**Participant environment**:
The MicroHack-managed environment allocated to one participant for the bootcamp day and retained across all six labs, without requiring six different sets of credentials.
_Avoid_: Lab environment (when implying a separate allocation per lab)

**Shared provisioning**:
The common setup of bootcamp resources within participant environments, performed through the MicroHack platform's own lab deployment and used by all six labs rather than separate provisioning for each lab.
_Avoid_: Provisioning interface (when implying a contract beyond the MicroHack platform's)

**Lab prerequisite**:
Anything a lab requires before its first participant action, whether from the participant environment or the attendee's workstation. Each lab states its own; an unstated prerequisite is an authoring fault in that lab.

**Shared baseline dataset**:
The common dataset available and searchable at the start of all six labs, without requiring any lab to be completed first.
